# Leave Out the Kettle

Entry for **The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue** — a 104-word
monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. His grand
speech to the hero keeps getting derailed by his own tower: a scrying mirror
that demands a password reset every third Tuesday, and a kettle that will
not wait for the conquest. The tiredness lives in the interruptions, the
pauses and the small deflections — he never once says he is weary — and the
sting lands in the last line, when he sends the hero home with orders to
tell the bards he was terrifying, and to leave out the kettle.

`main.py` writes the monologue to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`.
